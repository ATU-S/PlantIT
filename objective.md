Government hospitals frequently encounter operational challenges such as overcrowded 
outpatient departments, prolonged waiting times, fragmented patient coordination, and 
inefficient utilization of hospital resources. These issues are often caused by disconnected 
workflows across registration, appointments, consultation, laboratory services, pharmacy, and 
patient assistance, resulting in reduced service quality and patient satisfaction. This paper 
presents an Agentic AI-Based Hospital Operations and Patient Journey Management 
System, a patient-centric platform designed to streamline the complete outpatient care process 
through coordinated intelligent agents.  
The proposed system employs specialized AI agents to manage key hospital operations, 
including patient registration, appointment scheduling, department routing, queue prediction, 
laboratory coordination, pharmacy notifications, ambulance booking, and wheelchair 
assistance. A central orchestration agent coordinates communication among these specialized 
agents to optimize patient flow, improve resource utilization, and provide real-time operational 
insights. The platform supports both web and mobile applications, enabling patients to access 
hospital services before, during, and after their visit. Additionally, multilingual voice 
interaction, including Malayalam language support, enhances accessibility for a wider patient 
population.  
Unlike conventional Hospital Management Systems that primarily focus on administrative 
record management, the proposed solution acts as an intelligent operational coordination layer 
that assists hospital staff in making workflow decisions without participating in clinical 
diagnosis or treatment. Predictive analytics are utilized to estimate waiting times, identify 
departmental bottlenecks, and recommend workload balancing strategies, while ensuring that 
all clinical decisions remain under the supervision of healthcare professionals.  
The proposed framework aims to reduce outpatient waiting times, improve patient experience, 
enhance accessibility through transportation and mobility assistance, and increase the 
operational efficiency of government hospitals. By integrating agentic artificial intelligence 
with hospital workflow management, the system offers a scalable and extensible foundation 
for modernizing public healthcare operations while maintaining a patient-centred approach.
